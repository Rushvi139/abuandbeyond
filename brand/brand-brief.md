# Abu & Beyond — brand brief

Locked October 2026. Use this document as the source of truth for any creative, human-made or AI-generated.

---

## 1. What this is

A global kitchen in the Aravalli hills. Takeaway and delivery from Mount Abu, serving quick food inspired by different corners of the world: Mexican, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, burgers, sandwiches, and the local usual, upgraded.

Not fine dining. Not another dhaba. Loud, good food that feels a little unexpected in a hill station.

**The name.** Abu is where we are. Beyond is where we look. The ampersand is the whole idea: Abu *and* somewhere else, every order.

**Brand thought.** A little bit of the world, from the hills of Abu.

**Primary line.** Beyond the usual.

---

## 2. The two halves

Everything splits down the ampersand.

| Abu (place) | Beyond (food) |
|---|---|
| Guru Shikhar, 1722 m | Noodles, pasta, burritos, pita |
| Aravalli ridge | Taco, wrap, bread, chilli |
| Leopards, bhalu, paw prints | Steam, heat, the drop |
| Gaumukh, Dilwara, Nakki Lake | Mexico, Italy, Lebanon, China |
| Hill green | Clay red |

Place is quiet, old, and green. Food is loud, fast, and red. A creative should always have both in it somewhere, even if one is just the patch.

---

## 3. Logo

**The patch.** Hot clay rounded rectangle, thick ink keyline, ABU over a ridge line over BEYOND, "The global kitchen" beneath. Always tilted 4° anticlockwise when it is a sticker; straight when it is a signage or a wordmark lockup.

Files are in `public/brand/`. Use:
- `patch-primary` — default, on any light or kraft surface
- `patch-primary-tilted` — sticker use
- `patch-mono-white` — over dark photography or video
- `ampersand-seal-acid` / `favicon` — favicons, profile pictures, small sizes
- `wordmark-ink` — only where the patch physically cannot fit (a menu footer, a bill)

Never: recolour the patch outside the palette, remove the ridge line, stretch it, add a drop shadow, or put it on a gradient.

**Minimum size.** Patch 120 px wide on screen. Below that, use the ampersand seal.

**Clear space.** Half the height of the patch on all sides.

---

## 4. Colour

Four primaries carry every creative.

- **Ink** `#141414` — type, keylines, dark backgrounds
- **Hot clay** `#FF4D14` — the patch, the bar, the call to action
- **Acid** `#FFD400` — stickers, the seal, the one bright on a night layout
- **Bone** `#F3EFE6` — paper, reversed type

Secondaries: Hill green `#1F3A33`, Kraft `#D9C39C`, Clay red `#C0402A`, Gold `#C9A227` (hamper only), Night ink `#10161C`.

Pairings that work: ink on hot clay, ink on acid, bone on ink or night ink, acid as the only bright on night ink.
Avoid: hot clay type on acid (or the reverse), hill green on ink, gold anywhere but the hamper.

A layout is one background colour, one type colour, one accent. Never more.

---

## 5. Type

**Headlines.** Anton (Google Fonts). All caps, tight tracking, large. Two lines maximum; second line often in the accent colour.

**Everything else.** Helvetica Neue or Arial (Inter in the web app). Sentence case for sentences, caps with wide tracking only for small label lines.

No italics, no light weights, no serif anywhere.

---

## 6. Voice

Direct, a bit cheeky, local. Says the thing in six words. Humour comes from putting Abu next to somewhere far away, or from the hills themselves.

Lines we use:
- Beyond the usual.
- Nowhere higher. So we went further.
- Something comes down late at night. Usually it's us.
- Leopards sleep. We don't.
- Tacos in the hills.
- Pesto at 1722 metres.
- You came for the hills. Stay for the heat.
- Every direction from here is beyond.

Rules: never explain the joke. Never say "authentic", "fusion", "experience" or "journey". Numbers are good (1722 m, 670 km, 11 pm). Names of real places are good. Keep the Pandava reference as legend ("they say"), never as fact.

---

## 7. Packaging logic (why the app has cuisine stickers)

One plain box, one belly band with the patch, and a sticker that says where the food came from: ABU & ITALY, ABU & MEXICO, ABU & LEBANON, ABU & CHINA. In the app every dish carries the matching cuisine tag so the KOT tells the packer which sticker to use.
